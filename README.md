
# JanDrishti 🏙️

### Citizen-Verified Civic Issue Resolution Platform

> **Report. Resolve. Verify.**

JanDrishti is a web-based civic technology platform designed to improve transparency and accountability in the resolution of local civic issues.

The platform allows citizens to report civic problems with location and photographic evidence, while administrators can manage reported issues, update their status, and provide evidence of the action taken. Citizens can then verify whether the issue has actually been resolved.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Existing civic issue reporting systems primarily focus on collecting complaints and tracking their status. However, a complaint being marked as **"Resolved"** does not necessarily provide citizens with a simple mechanism to verify whether the problem was actually fixed.

JanDrishti focuses on the **resolution verification stage**.

The system creates a transparent workflow:

```text
Report Issue
     ↓
Assign / Review
     ↓
Take Action
     ↓
Upload Resolution Evidence
     ↓
Citizen Verification
     ↓
 ┌───────────────┐
 │               │
Verified       Not Fixed
 │               │
 ↓               ↓
Close        Reopen / Escalate
